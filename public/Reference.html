Substitute notes

  • Churchill: Instructions are on the teacher desk. Subs must be on campus 30 min. before the starting time of the 1st class being covered. Know that this job also requires for the sub to stay 30 min. after the class is finished.

    (1st half) The 1st class being covered begins at 8:55am and the last class being covered ends at 12:40pm (1:00pm on Thursdays). The subs hired for half a day are required to cover the entire 4 periods (half of a work day). Thank you!

    (2nd half) For afternoon assignments, subs must be on campus starting at 12:20pm. The 1st class being covered begins at 12:45pm (1:05pm on Thursdays) and the last class being covered ends at 4:25pm. Thank you!

    (full day) The 1st class being covered begins at 8:55am and the last class being covered ends at 4:25pm. Thank you!
